---
title: Label STEP MoU renewed at annual conference
date: 2025-12-27
kind: partnership
summary: The Memorandum of Understanding with the Swiss fair-trade organisation is renewed for another year, after eight camps across three districts in 2025.
---

The Memorandum of Understanding with Label STEP was renewed at the organisation's annual conference in December 2025.

Eight camps ran under the MoU during 2025, across three districts, focused on occupational health for weaving and labour communities.
