summary: RAVISH UCMD and Care Campaign PK sign a Memorandum of Understanding to widen free healthcare access in underserved communities.

RAVISH and Care Campaign PK have signed a Memorandum of Understanding, formalising a working relationship between the two organisations.

The partnership adds capacity to the free-camp programme — more hands, more medicines, and more communities reachable in a season.
