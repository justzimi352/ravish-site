title: The RAVISH Clinic opens
date: 2025-09-14
kind: milestone
summary: A permanent free clinic near Androon Lahore, established with human rights defender Tanveer Jahan. It runs weekly.
---

> The term 'Ravish' signifies a path, and this clinic embodies that concept — a pathway to dignity, healing, and hope. It represents more than merely a facility for treatment; it stands as a commitment that compassion and care will consistently be accessible to those who need it.

The clinic opened on 14 September 2025 at House #171, DAV Hostel #2, Dev Samaj Road, near Androon Lahore. It was established with Tanveer Jahan, a human rights defender and social development professional.

It runs weekly, offering consultations, follow-ups and basic diagnostics. Dr. Aashiq Hussain Wattoo has attended since the month it opened.

Its significance is structural rather than symbolic. Camps screen thousands of people for high blood pressure and high blood sugar. Until September 2025 there was nowhere free to send them. Now there is.
