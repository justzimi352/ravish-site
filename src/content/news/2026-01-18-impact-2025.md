IMPACT 2025 is out. The headline figures, all cumulative since March 2024:

15,000+ patients given medical care. 30,000+ one-time meals distributed. 55+ medical camps organised. PKR 3 million+ raised. 150+ activities conducted. 25+ major cities covered. 5,000+ volunteers registered. 40+ local cabinets established. 45+ partnerships enacted.

Set against IMPACT 2024, the direction is the story: funding up six-fold, activities up more than four-fold, patients treated up three-fold, cabinets up from 17 to more than 40.

The report also sets out where RAVISH intends to go next — expanding the clinic network, scaling occupational health work, strengthening disaster response, and improving data and reporting systems.
