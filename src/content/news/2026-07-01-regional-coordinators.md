---
title: Regional Coordinators introduced for 2026–27
date: 2026-07-01
kind: appointment
summary: A new tier between the National Cabinet and the 40+ local cabinets, created to mentor presidents and audit regional activity.
---

RAVISH has introduced a Regional Coordinator tier for the 2026–27 term.

Forty-plus cabinets is more than one national cabinet can mentor directly. Regional Coordinators sit between the two: they mentor local cabinet presidents, oversee operations in their region, audit activity and report upward.

Dr. Laiba Zahoor has been appointed Regional Coordinator for Lahore Suburban.
