---
title: KPK flood relief appeal opens
date: 2025-08-21
kind: campaign
summary: As the 2025 monsoon crisis escalated, RAVISH opened a fundraising appeal and mobilised relief teams into Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.
---

> The conversation reminded us that climate change is not tomorrow's threat — it's today's reality. Families are displaced, communities need urgent medical care, and your support can save lives.

The appeal funded what became the largest mobilisation in RAVISH's history: more than twenty flood relief camps across Punjab and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, a three-day deployment that treated over six thousand patients in Chiniot, Bhawana and Jhang, and a nine-day mission through Gilgit-Baltistan.
