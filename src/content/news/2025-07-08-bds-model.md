---
title: Cabinet model opens to dental students
date: 2025-07-08
kind: appointment
summary: A BDS model is added to the local cabinet structure, so dental students can hold cabinet roles alongside MBBS students.
---

Until July 2025 the local cabinet structure was built for MBBS students. A BDS model was added on 8 July 2025, opening cabinet roles to dental students.

The practical effect shows up in the camps: dental screening and consultation now run as a programme in their own right rather than an occasional add-on.
