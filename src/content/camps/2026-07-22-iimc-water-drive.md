> In the midst of scorching heat, even the smallest act of kindness can bring comfort, relief, and hope. Every bottle shared is a reminder that compassion is found in the simplest acts.

Heat is a medical emergency that does not present as one. The drive paired water distribution with heatstroke awareness — the early signs, and what to do before it becomes the kind of emergency that needs a hospital.
