Androon Lahore — the walled old city — is dense, poorly served, and only a few kilometres from some of the best hospitals in the country. Over three hundred patients were seen at this camp.

It is also where the RAVISH Clinic sits, which changed what this camp could offer. Patients whose blood pressure or blood sugar came back high were not simply told to see someone. They were told where, and when, and it was free.
