Between 13 and 15 September 2025, RAVISH teams treated more than six thousand patients across Chiniot, Bhawana and the villages of Tehsil Jorana near Jhang.

> We were treating patients right beside fields still submerged under floodwater — a sight that was both humbling and heartbreaking.

Mobile medical care, rations, hygiene kits and clothing went out together, because in a flood they are the same problem. Water-borne illness, skin infection and untreated chronic disease arrive at once, in people who have also lost the house they would have recovered in.

Dr. Alia Haider led the relief effort. Twenty or more flood relief camps ran across Punjab and KPK that monsoon, including in areas the brief for the season described only as "hard to reach".
