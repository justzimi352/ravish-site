The mission set out on 25 September 2025 and ran for nine days. Roads in the north were damaged or gone, which was both the reason the need was there and the reason nobody else had reached it.

Around five hundred patients were seen across the mission. Dr. Alia Haider led the climate and relief work, as she had through the KPK and Punjab flooding earlier in the season.
