Five hundred and thirty-seven people came through in a single day. It remains the highest patient count RAVISH has recorded at one camp, and it happened in the organisation's first year.

Consultations ran alongside blood pressure, blood glucose and BMI screening, with free medicines dispensed from an on-site pharmacy. The scale of the turnout was itself the finding: a community this size, this close to Lahore, with this much unmet need and no standing service to meet it.
