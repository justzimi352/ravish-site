A hundred and sixty patients through a partner clinic's OPD. Running inside a standing facility rather than a borrowed hall meant records, referral and follow-up were possible in a way a one-day camp in a courtyard cannot manage.

The lesson carried. Ten months later RAVISH opened premises of its own.
