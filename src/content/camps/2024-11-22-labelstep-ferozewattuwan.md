Carpet weaving is piece-rate work. Time away from the loom is unpaid, which means a clinic visit costs a family twice — the fare and the lost income — and so it does not happen. The injuries accumulate quietly: chronic back and neck pain, repetitive strain in the hands, eye strain from fine work in poor light.

This camp was designed around that. RAVISH ran it with Label STEP, the Swiss fair-trade organisation that works with handmade carpet producers, and with the Democratic Commission for Human Development.

The relationship was still informal at this stage. The formal Memorandum of Understanding followed in April 2025, and was renewed at Label STEP's annual conference that December.
