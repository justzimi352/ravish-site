Day three of the camp took us to Macharanwala and Syedabad in Tehsil Jorana, near Jhang.

We were treating patients right beside fields still submerged under floodwater — a sight that was both humbling and heartbreaking. The crop was gone. The house behind it was gone. And the man in front of us had walked to the camp with a skin infection from standing in that water for a week, apologising for taking up our time.

These camps humbled us in many ways.
