Ravish is humbled to have joined hands with Label STEP in organising a medical camp dedicated to the hardworking carpet weaver families.

Behind every masterpiece of craft lies the silent strength of these communities, and it was our privilege to extend care, compassion, and support to them.

What you learn at a camp like this is that the illness has a shape, and the shape is the work. Backs and necks that have been bent over a loom since childhood. Hands that no longer fully open. Eyes strained by years of fine knotting under a single bulb. None of it is mysterious, and almost none of it had been treated, because treatment costs a day's earnings and the earnings are counted by the knot.
