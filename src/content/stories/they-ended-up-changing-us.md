We came to make a difference, they ended up changing us.

There is something deeply humbling about being surrounded by so much light in a place that has seen its share of struggle. These children taught us more than we could ever teach them — about hope, about resilience, and about the beauty of simple kindness.

We came to give, but left feeling incredibly full. Sometimes all it takes is presence, love, and a little bit of time to create something truly meaningful.

This wasn't just a visit. It was a reminder of what really matters.
