A humbling experience at the medical camp. So grateful to be part of something that makes a difference.

Honestly, learned more in one day of real service than a week of lectures. You read about hypertension in a textbook and it is a number with a management plan attached. You take a blood pressure at a camp and it is a man who has been carrying that number for years, who has never been told, and who is now standing in front of you asking what it means.

That is not a thing a lecture can hand you.
